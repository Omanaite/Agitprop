# PRODUCT.md — Agitprop Studio Platform

## Register
brand (the /agitprop marketing page) + product (the /studio artist console)

## Users
Independent tattoo artists and other creatives who need a professional online presence. Technically non-expert. They care about how their portfolio looks, not how it works under the hood. Main pain: scattered DMs, no central booking, no portfolio that looks professional. Decision driver: trust and aesthetic credibility.

## Product Purpose
Agitprop gives artists a production-ready website, gallery, and booking system — with zero infrastructure work. Think "Squarespace for tattoo artists, but cheaper and with less noise."

## Brand Tone
Confident, dry, professional. No hype. No exclamation points. No "We believe every artist deserves...". Short sentences. Strong verbs. Let the product speak.

Anti-references: Mailchimp (too cheerful), Wix (too generic), any "rocket ship" / "supercharge" copy.

## Strategic Principles
- Free forever for the basics — never bait-and-switch
- One-time fee, never subscriptions (expanded storage)
- Build trust through honesty: tell users what costs what and why
- Admin/studio UI is functional-first (clarity > decoration)
- Public artist sites are aesthetic-first (each theme has a strong visual identity)

## Anti-patterns
- Gradient text (banned)
- Glassmorphism for its own sake
- 6 identical feature cards in a row (anti-card-overuse)
- Centered hero with stock illustration (banned)
- "Premium" / "Pro" upsell language
- Emoji in UI
